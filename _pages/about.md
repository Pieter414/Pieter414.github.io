I'm a final-year Computer Science student at [Brawijaya University](https://ub.ac.id) and currently the Lead Coordinator of the research assistants at the [Intelligent System Laboratory](https://labsc.ub.ac.id/). Most of my work sits where machine learning meets Indonesian language and healthcare, either checking whether medical vision-language models still hold up when you ask them in Bahasa Indonesia, develop training-free framework for translate video subtitle translation, instruction-tuning Indonesian LLMs, and graph neural networks for fraud detection. I also work part-time as an AI engineer at RAINS, building agentic knowledge-management tools.

Outside the lab I'm a data scientist at the [Basic Computing Community (BCC)](https://www.instagram.com/bccfilkom/) and a regular at data science competitions (Gemastik 2025 finalist).

**I'm open to research internships and data/AI internships.** Feel free to reach out by [email](mailto:pieterchristyan7@gmail.com).

## Research interests

- Multilingual robustness of medical vision-language models
- Instruction tuning and alignment of Indonesian LLMs
- Graph neural networks
- Applied computer vision and natural language processing, preferably Indonesian or SEA-based
